1. About Warranty Wallet

Warranty Wallet is an independent personal organization utility for recording warranty and purchase information, storing supporting documents, and scheduling expiration reminders. It is not a manufacturer, retailer, insurer, or warranty provider.

2. Information you add

You may enter product names, brands, models, serial numbers, sellers, categories, prices, currencies, purchase dates, warranty durations, notes, and reminder choices. You may attach receipt images, PDF warranty documents, and product photos. This content may include personal information.

The app stores this content locally in Android private app storage. AppFactoryDevelopment does not operate a Warranty Wallet account system, application backend, or automatic cloud-synchronization service for this content.

3. Reminders

Android WorkManager schedules local warranty-expiration reminders. Reminder settings remain on the device. Android controls notification permission and may delay background work.

4. Manual encrypted backups

You can create a password-protected backup containing warranty records, reminder choices, attachment metadata, and attachment bytes. Backups use password-derived authenticated encryption and are created only when you request them.

Android's document picker lets you choose local storage or an installed third-party document/cloud provider. A chosen provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Warranty Wallet does not automatically upload backups to Google Drive or another provider.

You must retain the file and password. Warranty Wallet and AppFactoryDevelopment cannot recover a forgotten password or guarantee that a provider will retain or recover a file.

5. Android backup and deletion risks

Android automatic app backup and device transfer are currently disabled for the database, preferences, and private files. Uninstalling, clearing app storage, losing the device, or device failure may permanently remove local data that was not manually backed up.

6. Google Play Billing

The optional one-time Pro purchase uses Google Play Billing to retrieve product information, initiate and acknowledge purchases, check ownership, and restore eligible purchases. Google processes the transaction under its terms and privacy policy. The app locally caches limited entitlement status. Pro ownership is checked through Google Play and is never imported from a backup.

7. Advertising and consent

The Free version may show an AdMob banner when production advertising is configured and applicable consent permits a request. Pro suppresses in-app ads. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable advertising privacy choices and a privacy-options entry point.

Depending on configuration, region, consent, device settings, and law, Google and participating advertising partners may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. AppFactoryDevelopment must verify the final AdMob, Privacy & messaging, partner, and SDK configuration before release.

8. Third-party services

These providers may process technical or account-related information independently under their terms. Review Google's Privacy Policy and the privacy policy of any selected document provider.

9. Sharing and Play disclosures

AppFactoryDevelopment does not receive locally stored warranty records through an app-owned backend because no such backend exists. User-initiated export is controlled by your provider choice. Third-party SDK processing must be reflected in the final Google Play Data Safety form. This policy does not claim that the app “collects no data.” AppFactoryDevelopment must confirm final collection, sharing, and sale declarations under applicable definitions.

10. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, replace them through restore, clear storage, or uninstall. Uninstalling does not delete backup files, Google Play transaction records, or provider-held information; delete those through the relevant provider.

11. Security

Local data uses Android private app storage. Portable backups are encrypted and integrity-protected. No system can guarantee absolute security, uninterrupted availability, or recovery.
